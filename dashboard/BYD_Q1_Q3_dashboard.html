How to use this dashboard

  • Quarterly management review: keep P3 → P4, choose a segment, and read the Capture Gap and the brands that gained share against their own benchmark.
  • Persistence check: switch to earlier comparisons to see whether a pattern held across P1 → P2 and P2 → P3 before acting on it.
  • Monthly monitoring: after each pipeline refresh, the monthly charts show whether BYD's share in the priority segment is recovering.

Data: analysis mart of the MySQL database byd_bev_sg (segment_month_full and fact_registration), built from LTA data via data.gov.sg. Periods run August to July: P1 = Aug 2022 to Jul 2023, P2 = Aug 2023 to Jul 2024, P3 = Aug 2024 to Jul 2025, P4 = Aug 2025 to Jul 2026. Field definitions are in data_dictionary.md.
